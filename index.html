Our Story

Since 2018, Mushka Pizza has been bringing authentic Italian flavors to our community. Our master pizzaiolos use traditional techniques passed down through generations, combined with the finest local ingredients.

Every pizza is handcrafted with love, from the perfectly fermented dough to the carefully selected toppings. We believe that great pizza brings people together, creating moments of joy and connection.
